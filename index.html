About关于我们

We are an early-stage investor. We look for frontier technology and creative people before most others notice them, invest capital and our own time at the very beginning, and then hold for the long term so that value has room to grow.我们是一家早期投资机构。我们在多数人注意到之前，寻找前沿科技和有创造力的人；在最早的阶段投入资本，也投入我们自己的时间；然后长期持有，给价值足够的时间成长。

What we invest in投资方向

What we stay with我们投什么

Seventy percent, twenty, ten. The split is the plain part. Under it are the things, and the people, we are willing to keep company with.资金按七成、两成、一成分配。数字只是结构。我们真正在意的，是愿意长期同行的那些方向和人。

70%

Advanced technology先进科技

Most of the capital sits here, while the work is still early. Models. The chain that has to grow around them. Industries that do not have a name yet. We arrive before it is obvious, and we stay through the quiet years.七成资金投向先进科技，而且是在还早、还不热闹的时候：大模型，以及围绕它生长的产业链，还有尚在形成中的未来产业。我们愿意先到场，也愿意在低谷里继续支持。

20%

Advanced consumer先进消费

A smaller share is for the way a day is lived. A brand a person comes back to. A tool that belongs in the hand. A workshop that will not pass a careless part. We are backing that care.两成投向先进消费：让人愿意反复选择的品牌，真正进入日常生活的智能硬件，以及在细节上不肯将就的高端制造。我们看重的，是把事情做好的那份认真。

10%

Independent creators独立创作者

The rest is for people who sign the work themselves. An artist, a musician, a craftsperson, a game studio, a watchmaker. The work may be small. We still sit with it for a long time.一成留给独立创作者：为自己作品署名的艺术家、音乐人、手艺人，以及独立游戏工作室和制表师。作品不必很大，我们选择长期支持。
