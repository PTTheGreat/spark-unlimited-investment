## Sophia Su (苏瑜)

Identity, matched across sources:

- Partner on this site. Chinese name on the page: 苏瑜（Sophia）. Earlier bio: CFO of Zhidemai Technology (值得买 / SMZDM); former KPMG Huazhen partner, vice president of finance at Chehaoduo (车好多), CFO of Zhonghe Yunke (众合云科); independent director and audit-committee chair at MetaLight (元光科技) and Pinecone (松果出行); master’s degree from Peking University.
- The same biography is in the company filing [北京值得买科技股份有限公司 关于变更首席财务官的公告, 29 June 2026](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-06-29/1225395083.PDF) (公告编号 2026-033) and in MetaLight Inc.’s 2025 annual report on HKEX (stock code 02605), which names Ms. Su Yu (蘇瑜) as independent non-executive director and audit-committee chairperson.
- Not a same-name guess: the filing, the HKEX report, and the portrait caption below all use 苏瑜 together with the Zhonghe CFO role and the KPMG / 车好多 career.
